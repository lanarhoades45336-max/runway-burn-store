02 — What you get

Two scripts that tell you the truth.

No dashboards to log into, no spreadsheets to maintain. Drop the files next to your money, run them, get a straight answer.

01

runway.py

Give it your cash, your monthly cost and your income. It prints your runway in days and months, and can Monte-Carlo your odds of surviving a horizon when your income is irregular.

$ python3 runway.py --cash 12000 \
    --monthly-cost 4500 --monthly-income 1000
  net / month:     -3,500.00
  runway:          103 days (~3.4 months)
02

subscriptions.py

Point it at a CSV of transactions (date, description, amount). It groups recurring charges, annualises them, and shows the yearly cost of every subscription quietly eating your runway.

$ python3 subscriptions.py --csv statement.csv
  recurring services:  3
  monthly total:       83.99
  annualized:          1,007.88
    - Cloud Storage: 39.00/mo (2x)
03
