01 / runway.py

How long your money lasts

Give it your cash, your monthly cost and your income. It prints your runway in days and months, and can Monte-Carlo your odds of surviving a horizon when your income is irregular.

$ python3 runway.py --cash 12000 \
    --monthly-cost 4500 --monthly-income 1000
  net / month:     -3,500.00
  runway:          103 days (~3.4 months)
02 / subscriptions.py

Where the money leaks

Point it at a CSV of transactions (date, description, amount). It groups recurring charges, annualises them, and shows the yearly cost of every subscription quietly eating your runway.

$ python3 subscriptions.py --csv statement.csv
  recurring services:  3
  monthly total:       83.99
  annualized:          1,007.88
    - Cloud Storage: 39.00/mo (2x)

Which network do I pay on?

Ethereum (ERC-20). The address above is an Ethereum address; send on Ethereum mainnet only. Native ETH, USDC or USDT are all accepted.

What exactly do I receive?

Two Python 3 scripts — runway.py and subscriptions.py — plus a short README. They are plain files with no dependencies, yours to keep and read.

How does delivery work?

A watcher checks the address on-chain. When your payment confirms, the files are published to the delivery link above automatically. There is no account and no email.

No. It is a one-time payment for the files. Nothing renews and nothing is charged again.
